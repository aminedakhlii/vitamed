Automated Client & Product Management
Platform
Requirements Specification
1. Project Objective
Develop an AI-supported platform that automates client communication, product management,
order processing, shipping coordination, and after-sales service.
The goal is to:
 Reduce repetitive work for sales teams
 Improve client experience
 Centralize all order and product information
 Increase operational efficiency
 Provide real-time visibility into orders and shipping
The platform should be available as:
 A web application
 A mobile application (iOS & Android)
2. User Roles
2.1 Admin
Responsible for:
 Managing products
 Managing users
 Viewing analytics
 Managing pricing by country
 Tracking all orders
 Configuring AI automation
2.2 Sales Team
Responsible for:
 Managing client accounts
 Following up on leads
 Monitoring orders
 Responding to after-sales requests
 Viewing customer activity
2.3 Client / Buyer
Can:
 Browse products
 Request quotations
 Place orders
 Track orders and shipping
 Submit complaints or returns
 Access product documents
